Is this PC ready?

RAM, disk, Ollama and the local model. Nothing leaves this PC.

Which apps may HCWMA open without asking?

Tick the apps HCWMA may open whenever you ask. Any other app shows "Allow once / Always allow / Deny" first. This is about opening only: clicks, typing and sends inside an app always ask you. Blocked apps (browsers, passwords, banking, terminals, system tools, app stores) are not listed.

Adapters

An adapter lets an agent use one app or service through its own careful tools. They ship with HCWMA; nothing is downloaded. Turn on only the ones you want.

Folders HCWMA may read

Where Windows really keeps your folders (OneDrive may move Desktop and Documents). Added folders are used after HCWMA restarts.
